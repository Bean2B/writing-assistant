# 交流约定 / Community conduct

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

- 尊重参与者，围绕具体问题讨论，避免人身攻击和骚扰。
- 用清楚的复现步骤或使用场景说明问题和建议。
- 保护隐私，不发布私人稿件、凭据或他人的个人信息。
- 引用他人的工作时说明来源，不将自己的 fork 冒充官方项目。

维护者可以移除不符合上述约定的内容。问题反馈入口为 [Issues](https://github.com/Bean2B/writing-assistant/issues)。

## English

- Respect participants. Discuss the issue without personal attacks or harassment.
- Describe problems and suggestions with clear reproduction steps or use cases.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish private manuscripts, credentials or another person's personal information.
- Credit others' work and do not present your fork as the official project.

Maintainers may remove content that violates these guidelines. Use [Issues](https://github.com/Bean2B/writing-assistant/issues) for feedback.
