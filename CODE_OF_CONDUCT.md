# 社区行为 / Community conduct

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

请尊重他人，用合成证据说明具体问题，不发布私人材料、不骚扰他人、不把自己的
fork 冒充官方项目。维护者决定是否采纳建议并实现官方变更。
许可证授予后的开源权利与官方写入权限各自独立。

## English

Be respectful and report concrete problems with synthetic evidence. Do not post
private material, harass others, or represent your fork as the official project.
Maintainers decide whether to accept a suggestion and implement official changes.
Open-source license rights, once granted, are separate from official write access.
