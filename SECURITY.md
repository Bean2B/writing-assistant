# 安全说明 / Security

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

### 报告问题

请在 [Issues](https://github.com/Bean2B/writing-assistant/issues) 提供可以安全公开的最小复现：应用版本、操作系统、相关命令、预期结果和实际结果。使用虚构文本替代真实稿件。

Issues 公开可见。不要附上凭据、个人信息、私密稿件、完整诊断包，或可能暴露他人数据的内容。不能安全公开的细节请勿发布。

### 使用时保护数据

创作数据和诊断保存在本地，由本机文件权限保护。AI 任务会通过 Codex 使用外部服务；项目不收集这些数据。共享日志前自行检查内容，升级或移动项目之前先备份。

参阅[隐私说明](docs/privacy.md)、[Codex 支持](docs/codex-support.md)和[备份与恢复](docs/usage.md)。

## English

### Report a problem

Open an [issue](https://github.com/Bean2B/writing-assistant/issues) with a minimal reproduction that is safe to publish: application version, operating system, relevant command, expected result and actual result. Replace real manuscripts with fictional text.

Issues are public. Do not attach credentials, personal information, private manuscripts, full diagnostic bundles or material that could expose another person's data. Leave out details that cannot safely be public.

### Protect your data

Creative data and diagnostics are stored locally under your file permissions. AI tasks use an external service through Codex; this project does not collect that data. Inspect logs before sharing them, and back up before upgrading or moving a project.

See [Privacy](docs/privacy.md#english), [Codex support](docs/codex-support.md#english) and [backup and recovery](docs/usage.md#english).
