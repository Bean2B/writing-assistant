# Lotus writing method

Build the current writing situation around a continuing story engine, character desires, relationships, pressures and open questions. Expand connected areas when they matter to the present action. Do not prescribe inevitable character choices, a fixed chapter order or a predetermined ending.

Use current established events and explicitly selected candidate materials. A plan, hope or prediction is not an event. Preserve the source and limits of each character's knowledge. Carry bodily costs and relationship consequences into subsequent scenes. Keep full selected prior prose available for voice and continuity.

Give a writer the permitted current scene and space to choose. Do not supply hidden future answers, other characters' private knowledge or unfiltered simulation branches. Simulation is optional; its absence does not prevent scene preparation or drafting.

When a review or revision is explicitly requested, bind it to the actual draft and relevant inputs. Independent readers judge the text they receive rather than an author's explanation. Preserve original drafts and unresolved disagreements. Structural checks and internal review do not establish reader satisfaction or content adoption.

Only the requested phase is prepared. This component does not schedule a later phase, adopt prose or update a story's formal facts.
