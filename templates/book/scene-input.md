# Scene worksheet

- Whose choice changes the situation, what do they privately want, and what alternative is available?
- Which other person would interpret the same event differently?
- Which action or exchange of an object must be clear on the first reading?
- What should the reader perceive now, and what may they remember, misinterpret or learn later?
- Which earlier event genuinely affects the decision? Attach a chapter, revision and short quote when needed.
- Where should the moment linger, and what can the reader infer across the cut?

Use whichever questions resolve a difficulty. Keep author-only answers outside the reader packet.
