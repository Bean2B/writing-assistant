# Volume or turning-point outline

Use the center for the live story question. Place selected consequences, interests or possible turns around it. An important outer cell can become the center of a later grid. Leave dormant threads with a cue for the circumstances that might revive them.

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What the protagonist wants in private | Someone who needs a different outcome | A first success and the obligation it creates |
| A relationship that could change | **Center — the choice or unresolved desire** | What makes withdrawal costly |
| A skill tested under changed conditions | A loss, delay or unfulfilled promise | A consequence the reader could anticipate |

Choose cells that earn a place in this story. At a major turn ask what the protagonist wants more now, what they are reluctant to lose, and what they actually choose or refuse. Record a significant change as a book note if it matters beyond this scene.
